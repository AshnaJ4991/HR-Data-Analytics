## 📌 Overview

This project is an interactive **Power BI dashboard** that analyses employee data to understand **workforce composition and attrition**. It brings headline HR metrics and detailed breakdowns into a single page, so HR teams can quickly see where employees are leaving and why it might be happening.

The dashboard supports filtering by **department** and **age group**, and every chart updates instantly.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Employee attrition is costly, and HR teams often lack a clear view of which departments, roles, age groups and salary bands are most affected. Raw spreadsheets make these patterns hard to spot.

This dashboard answers questions such as:
- How many employees have left, and what is the overall attrition rate?
- Which departments and job roles see the most attrition?
- Does attrition vary by salary slab, age group, gender or experience?
- How does job satisfaction relate to attrition within each role?

## ✨ Dashboard Features

- **Attrition by Department** – donut chart showing each department's share of attrition
- **Attrition by Salary Slab** – compares employees who stayed and left across salary bands
- **Attrition by Job Role and Satisfaction** – matrix of attrition counts by job role and satisfaction rating (1 to 4)
- **Attrition by Gender** – male and female split of attrition
- **Attrition Trend by Experience** – area chart of attrition across total years of experience
- **Age Group Distribution** – employee count by age group
- **Department-wise Employee Count** – funnel chart of headcount per department
- **Interactive slicers** for Department and Age Group

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- The overall attrition rate is **16.3%** (231 of 1,417 employees).
- **Administration** accounts for the largest share of attrition (84 employees, about 36%).
- **Laboratory Technician** is the job role with the highest attrition count (59).
- **Operations** is the largest department by headcount (512 employees).
- The **26–35** age group is the largest part of the workforce (588 employees).
